## Runtime additions

The Linux-style training desktop, virtual filesystem and shell commands, full-screen pause/campus guide, mission content, door interactions and cream-entrance repair are project additions. The hinged and sliding glass entrances, aligned landings/stairs, reception floor connection, full-plinth floor collision and canopy underside details are created at runtime. The campus Blender source, exported campus GLB and user-supplied source model files remain unchanged by these runtime edits.
